# Principles

## 1. The queue is the work
The quote list is the main surface; everything else serves deciding who to chase next.
- In practice: default sort puts overdue, then due today, first; one primary action per row.
- Exceptions: the "no follow-ups due" empty state may carry an illustration.
- What this rules out: chart dashboards above the list; more than one filled button per row.

## 2. Say exactly what happens
Labels name the real effect: "Open Messages app", "Copy message", "Call customer". Never "Send" when nothing is sent in-app.
- In practice: external actions leave the quote waiting for the staff member to record the outcome.
- What this rules out: implying a text was sent when an external app was only opened.

## 3. Quiet surfaces, clear state
Separation comes from 1px borders; colour is reserved for state (success, attention) and the single accent.
- In practice: statuses are text on a tinted background.
- What this rules out: gradients, decorative shadows on cards, a second accent colour.

## 4. Never lose the user's place
Opening, saving and closing preserve search, filters, selection and scroll.
- In practice: a saved quote that leaves the filter stays open until closed or "Next quote".
- What this rules out: resetting filters after an action; jumping scroll on close.

## 5. Readable numbers
Money and counts use tabular numerals, right-aligned in tables.
- What this rules out: proportional digits in totals; centred currency.
